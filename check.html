Free Readiness Check

Answer 18 short questions. You see your score and your top 3 gaps as soon as you finish, and a copy goes to your email.

About you

Your organization

1. Is your organization a registered 501(c)(3)?
2. Is your mission statement written and approved by your board?
3. Is your IRS and state filing status current (990 filed, state registration active)?
4. Do you run at least one program with a defined group served and a schedule?
5. Do you track how many people you serve?
6. Do you measure results beyond counts (before and after, outcomes)?
7. Do you have a board-approved annual budget?
8. Could you pull up last year's income and expenses within a day?
9. Do you track restricted and unrestricted money separately?
10. How many voting board members do you have who aren't related to each other?
11. How often does your board meet with written minutes?
12. Does your board have a written conflict of interest policy, signed each year?
13. Do you keep core documents in one shared place (bylaws, IRS letter, budget, board list, 990)?
14. Do you have a written overview of your organization that you reuse in applications?
15. Has a CPA audited or reviewed your financial statements?
16. Who would prepare a funding application?
17. Have you applied for a grant before?
18. Do you keep a list of funders and deadlines you plan to apply to?
