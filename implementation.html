How to book

  1. Pick a package: Starter, Core, or Full.
  2. Give us 3 times you're free in the next 14 days.
  3. Pay at checkout. If you booked within 30 days of your assessment, the $497 credit comes off your price.
  4. Our team contacts you to confirm a start time and your fix list.

How it works

  1. You choose which fixes to start with.
  2. We agree on scope, price, and dates in writing.
  3. We meet for 30 minutes each week and build the work in shared folders. Projects run 2 to 10 weeks, depending on how many fixes you choose.
  4. You approve each finished item, and we close with a handoff session.

Who can buy this
