About Plumbwren

Plumbwren helps small nonprofits get their organization in order before they ask for money. We review what a funder will look at, tell you where you stand, and help you fix the gaps. We don't write grants, and we don't promise funding. We focus on the work that makes your organization easier to trust.

Every report goes through two quality checks before it reaches you.

FAQ

Is the free check really free?

Yes. You don't enter payment details.

Do you write grants?

No. We help you get ready to apply.

Will this get us funded?

We can't promise that. Funders decide. We help you fix what might stop them from saying yes.

Who is this for?

Small nonprofits, especially those with few or no paid staff.

Is this legal or accounting advice?

No. For legal or tax questions, talk to a licensed attorney or CPA.
